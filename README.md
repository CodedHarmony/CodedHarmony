# Rhayn Jooste

**Learning design and AI fluency, backed by four years as a music examiner.**

For four years I was an examiner (RSL Awards), holding an assessment standard so that a candidate's mark did not depend on who was examining that day. I've also built and run Classical Guitar Rocks for the past eleven years, a self-produced WordPress and YouTube education platform where I own every stage, from research and scripting to publication and audience analytics.

Now I'm applying both to digital learning with AI-assisted workflows, and I write up the working as I go, including what I had to correct.

## Current work

**[n8n-job-search-template](https://github.com/CodedHarmony/n8n-job-search-template)**
A self-hosted n8n workflow that watches job boards on a schedule, screens the results with a local LLM and emails the matches. If the LLM is unreachable, every job is still sent with a warning banner, so a broken screening layer gets louder rather than quieter.

## Computers

I wrote my first code in BASIC in the 1980s and saved it to floppy disc. I was lucky enough to have access to some of the first laptops, which look more like desktop towers now, and I played games in monochrome. Even then I was messing around with sounds and key bindings, and generally having fun with ones and zeros.

These days I love Python. I've never been keen on Java, although I did enjoy building applets with it. I've built my own websites along the way, and now I mostly use AI to help me build the programs I need.

## Music

Music sits at the heart of everything I do. I've spent two decades designing and delivering curricula, from one-to-one guitar tuition to whole-class programmes for a national music charity, and I led the classical guitar curriculum at the Junior Conservatoire of the Royal Welsh College of Music and Drama.
